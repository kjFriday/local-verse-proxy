The base URL for every seat, with its own key card. A guess at this machine's address: if it is wrong, start Front Desk with --seat-url.

A key card is a seat's own key: it works only through the proxy, for its models, up to its budget, until it expires. Some budgets refill on a schedule. A seat is one person, computer or app.

Make a key card

One person, computer or app. Letters, digits, dots, dashes and underscores; no spaces.

Models it may use

The doors this card opens. Names come from config.yaml.

The most it may spend: in all, or between refills if you pick one below. Models that run on your own Ollama cost nothing.

With a refill the budget comes back in full soon after midnight UTC (world time), on Mondays, or on the 1st. Each card shows when, in your own time. With Never, to top up, cancel the card and make a new one.

After this the card stops working for good.

The tally is what each seat used and what it cost. Tokens are the word pieces AI models count and charge by. Refused requests are ones the proxy turned down, such as a model the card may not use, or a card over its budget; they count as requests. The proxy saves the tally in batches, so new use can take a minute to show.
